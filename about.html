Our Story

From a Vision to Bangalore's Trust

Kaivalya Properties & Builders was founded in 2024 with a simple but powerful vision: to make quality real estate accessible, transparent, and genuinely rewarding for every buyer in Bangalore.

We deal in lands, buildings, and construction — covering every aspect of the real estate journey from raw land acquisition to move-in-ready homes. What sets us apart is our relentless commitment to legal clarity, construction quality, and client relationships that last beyond the sale.

Every plot we develop and every building we construct carries the Kaivalya promise — quality you can see, trust you can feel, and value that grows with time.

Our Journey

How We've Grown

Nov 2024

Company Founded

Kaivalya Properties & Builders incorporated in Bangalore with a mission to transform real estate with transparency and quality.

Dec 2024

First Land Project

Launched Kaivalya Prime Acres in Devanahalli — 80 BDA-approved plots with full infrastructure. Sold out in record time.

May 2025

RERA Registration

All active projects registered under Karnataka RERA from day one, ensuring complete buyer protection and legal clarity.

Jun 2025

Appartment Project Launch

Launched Kaivalya Green Acres — 5 premium 2 & 1 BHK Flats in Bagaluru Cross, attracting Bangalore's discerning homebuyers.

2026

Scaling Across Bangalore

Expanding into Whitefield, Electronic City, and North Bangalore with apartments, commercial spaces, and larger villa townships.
